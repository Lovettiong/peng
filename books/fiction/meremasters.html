Book 3 of the Mind Masters Trilogy

Mere Masters

To save Tekrem, one must master the self.

Pit is a mind reader- he hears the thoughts of others. As a mind-writer, Cei can command thoughts.

As Claire assumes the role of Town Leader, a gang war erupts- throwing Tekrem into chaos. Despite their best efforts, the violence escalates and spreads. Claire finds the town on the edge of a civil war.

Under the turmoil, however, a much more dangerous threat crawls and feeds. To save the city, Pit and Cei need to be masters themselves.
